## The event
- "Build an agent that does real work." Fully online, 22–27 Oct 2026. Prize pool up to $20,000.
- Teams of 1–5 builders; solo allowed. Open worldwide, 16+. Free to enter.
- Build window: Oct 22, 00:00 UTC → Oct 27, 23:45 UTC (hard deadline).
- Results announced: Nov 6, 2026, 16:00 UTC.
- Organiser: GenAI Works. Judges/mentors include Michael Hay (CTO, Zetaris),
  Michael Armentano (SpaceXAI), Sam Cummings / Shestakov Vladyslav / Remus Ranca (GenAI Works).

## Track 03 — Reasoning Architecture (our track)
> "Demonstrate a novel ability to turn data, knowledge, memory, and reasoning
> into reliable decisions and actions."

Prize: $3,600 — 1st $1,500, 2nd $1,200, 3rd $900.

## Scoring — 100 points (official)
| Criterion | Pts | What it means (official wording) |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | 30 | Consistency with the challenge statements: how effectively the solution addresses a meaningful real-world problem for its intended users. |
| Technical | 20 | How well the solution is built: technical implementation, reliability, architecture. |
| Innovation | 15 | How original/creative the approach is. |
| Demo | 15 | How clearly the demo shows the solution, functionality, and value to intended users. |
| Product UX | 10 | How intuitive, accessible, easy to use for intended users. |
| Sponsor Tech | 10 | How effectively the solution uses the sponsors' technology. |
